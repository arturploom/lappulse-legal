Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 10, 2026

LapPulse ("we", "our", or "the app") is a motorsport lap timing application for iOS. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data LapPulse collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Location Data (GPS)

LapPulse accesses your device's location only while the app is in use (When In Use permission). GPS coordinates are used to:

  • Calculate lap times, speed, and sector splits
  • Detect start/finish line crossings
  • Render track maps and racing lines
  • Auto-detect nearby tracks

Location data is processed locally on your device. GPS coordinates are stored in session files on your device only — they are never uploaded to our servers.

Bluetooth Data

LapPulse connects to external GPS receivers (e.g., RaceBox Mini) via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). BLE is used solely to receive high-frequency GPS telemetry data. We do not scan for or interact with any other Bluetooth devices.

Session Data

Lap times, telemetry samples, track layouts, and session metadata are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly choose to share or export a session file.

Crash Reports

LapPulse uses Firebase Crashlytics (by Google), a third-party error monitoring service, to collect crash reports. When the app crashes, the following information may be sent to Firebase:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and build number
  • Stack traces and error messages
  • General device state (memory usage, battery level)

Crash reports do not include GPS coordinates, session data, personal information, or any telemetry recorded during your sessions. Crash data is used solely to identify and fix bugs.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no ad networks)
  • We do not collect or store your location data on any server
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
  • We do not require account creation

3. Data Storage & Sharing

All session and track data is stored locally on your device. You may choose to:

  • Export sessions as .lappulse or .csv files via the iOS share sheet
  • Share sessions with other LapPulse users via AirDrop, Messages, or other apps

These actions are entirely user-initiated. We have no access to shared files.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Open-Meteo (weather data) — Free weather API used to tag sessions with weather conditions. Only your approximate location (latitude/longitude) is sent to fetch current weather. Open-Meteo Terms

5. Children's Privacy

LapPulse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete sessions at any time within the app
  • Revoke permissions (Location, Bluetooth) via iOS Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
