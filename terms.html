2. Description of the Service

LapPulse is a GPS-based lap timing and telemetry application designed primarily for kart racing. The App provides:

  • Lap timing and telemetry recording using GPS data at up to 25 Hz (via compatible RaceBox Bluetooth Low Energy devices) or via the device's built-in GPS.
  • Session recording with telemetry data including speed, g-forces, heading, and track position.
  • Track creation and management for defining start/finish lines and track layouts.
  • Track Workshop for browsing, publishing, and downloading community-created track layouts.
  • Leaderboards for comparing lap times with other users.
  • Social features including user profiles, a follow system, and content sharing.
Important

LapPulse is not an official timing system. GPS-based timing is subject to measurement errors due to satellite signal quality, device hardware, environmental conditions, and other factors. Lap times should be treated as estimates and must not be relied upon for official competition results.
